About Nike

Nike is a globally recognized sportswear brand that has become a symbol of innovation, performance, and inspiration. Founded with a vision to bring inspiration and innovation to every athlete in the world, Nike designs and develops high-quality footwear, apparel, and sports equipment for people of all ages and skill levels. The brand focuses on combining advanced technology with modern style to create products that enhance comfort, durability, and performance. From professional athletes to everyday fitness enthusiasts, Nike products are built to support movement, speed, and confidence in every step. Over the years, Nike has continued to push boundaries through creative designs, powerful marketing, and continuous innovation, making it one of the most trusted and influential sports brands in the world.
